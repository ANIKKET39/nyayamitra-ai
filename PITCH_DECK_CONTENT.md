## Slide 2 — The Problem
**Legal empowerment in India is broken by complexity, not by law.**

- Over 1.4 billion citizens are entitled to legal rights and government schemes they don't know exist.
- RTI applications, complaint letters, and scheme applications require legal literacy most citizens don't have.
- Existing government portals are fragmented, jargon-heavy, and not conversational.
- A citizen with a valid grievance often gives up simply because they don't know **where to start**.

## Slide 3 — Our Solution
**NyayaMitra AI — a single AI-powered platform for civic & legal empowerment.**

Four integrated tools:
1. **Rights Navigator** — conversational AI that explains your rights in plain English/Hindi/Punjabi
2. **RTI Draft Generator** — auto-generates legally formatted RTI applications as PDFs
3. **Scheme Eligibility Checker** — matches citizens to government schemes they qualify for
4. **Complaint Letter Generator** — drafts professional complaint letters for consumer, landlord, workplace, and municipal issues

## Slide 5 — Tech Architecture
**Built on a modern, production-style stack.**

- **Frontend:** React + Vite + Tailwind CSS — responsive, animated, professional UI
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python) — clean, modular REST architecture (routes / services / prompts / data)
- **Database:** SQLite + SQLAlchemy ORM — Users, RTI Documents, Complaints, Eligibility History
- **AI Layer:** Claude API with dedicated prompt templates per feature, with a rule-based fallback engine for 100% offline reliability
- **PDF Generation:** ReportLab — branded, print-ready legal documents
